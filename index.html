The Story

The Method Behind the Madness

I'm a mechanical engineer who got lost in data — and never looked back. Pursuing a dual degree at BITS Pilani (MSc Chemistry + BE Mechanical Engineering), I discovered that the most interesting engineering problems aren't about machines — they're about information.

"Most tutorials show you how to call an API. I want to understand how to build the infrastructure that makes AI work reliably at scale."

At Flipkart, I work on seller funnel analytics and search personalization, turning behavioral data into product decisions. Outside work, I'm building IntelliRAG — a production-grade RAG platform from scratch, because the best way to understand a system is to build every piece of it yourself.

I care about correctness over convenience, documented tradeoffs over hand-waving, and systems that fail gracefully when things go wrong.
